How it works

取引のルール

Every anime film and show is a stock. You start the season with ₳100,000 of play money.

Prices follow real fandom — what people are watching, adding and talking about. Big trades move prices too, and breaking news moves them fast. Check the wire.

Tokyo rules: ▲ red is up, indigo is down ▼. Tickers can only move ±10% a day — hit the limit and they flag S高 or S安.

Films are blue chips. Classics are steady. Airing shows are wild — a bad episode is a red candle (well, indigo). Seasons reset every three months with the anime calendar. Leaderboard glory is forever.

₳ is never real money and never cashable. The flex is the prize.
